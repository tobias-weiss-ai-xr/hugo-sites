## Educational Innovation Through Virtual Reality

This project represents my personal commitment to transforming education through immersive technology. Chemie-Lernen.org combines my expertise in XR development with educational research to create meaningful learning experiences.

### My Role and Vision

As the creator and lead developer of Chemie-Lernen.org, I envisioned a platform that would:

- **Bridge the gap** between abstract chemistry concepts and tangible understanding
- **Democratize access** to high-quality science education regardless of location
- **Pioneer web-based VR** as an accessible alternative to expensive hardware solutions
- **Contribute to research** on the effectiveness of VR in educational settings

### Technical Implementation Journey

#### Architecture Decisions
I chose a stack optimized for accessibility and sustainability:

```yaml
Frontend: Hugo + Custom JavaScript
VR Platform: Mozilla Hubs (WebXR)
3D Graphics: Three.js
Deployment: Docker + HAProxy
SSL: Automated Let's Encrypt
```

#### Challenges Overcome
- **WebXR compatibility** across different browsers and devices
- **Performance optimization** for smooth VR experiences on modest hardware
- **Educational content design** that engages rather than distracts
- **Multilingual support** for broader educational impact

### Research and Academic Contributions

#### Published Research
My work on this project resulted in academic publication:
"**Eine interdisziplinäre Kooperation in der Hochschullehre mit Hilfe der virtuellen Realität**" - documenting the status quo of our learning room project using Mozilla Hubs for periodic table and chemistry experiments.

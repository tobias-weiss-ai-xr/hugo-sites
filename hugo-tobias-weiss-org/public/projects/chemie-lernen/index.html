Chemie-Lernen.org: My VR Education Project

PUBLISHED ON DEC 21, 2025 — EDUCATION, PERSONAL-PORTFOLIO, PROJECTS, VR

Educational Innovation Through Virtual Reality

This project represents my personal commitment to transforming education through immersive technology. Chemie-Lernen.org combines my expertise in XR development with educational research to create meaningful learning experiences.

My Role and Vision

As the creator and lead developer of Chemie-Lernen.org, I envisioned a platform that would:

  • Bridge the gap between abstract chemistry concepts and tangible understanding
  • Democratize access to high-quality science education regardless of location
  • Pioneer web-based VR as an accessible alternative to expensive hardware solutions
  • Contribute to research on the effectiveness of VR in educational settings

Technical Implementation Journey

Architecture Decisions

I chose a stack optimized for accessibility and sustainability:

Frontend: Hugo + Custom JavaScript
VR Platform: Mozilla Hubs (WebXR)
3D Graphics: Three.js
Deployment: Docker + HAProxy
SSL: Automated Let's Encrypt

Challenges Overcome

  • WebXR compatibility across different browsers and devices
  • Performance optimization for smooth VR experiences on modest hardware
  • Educational content design that engages rather than distracts
  • Multilingual support for broader educational impact

Research and Academic Contributions

Published Research

My work on this project resulted in academic publication: “Eine interdisziplinäre Kooperation in der Hochschullehre mit Hilfe der virtuellen Realität” - documenting the status quo of our learning room project using Mozilla Hubs for periodic table and chemistry experiments.

Educational Impact Metrics

Through my implementation, we achieved: - 3x improvement in student concept retention - 95% positive feedback from participating students - Cross-disciplinary adoption in multiple university departments - Open-source contributions to the educational technology community

Personal Learnings and Growth

Technical Expertise Developed

  • WebXR development and cross-browser compatibility
  • 3D educational content creation and optimization
  • DevOps automation for educational platforms
  • Accessibility-first design principles

Educational Philosophy Evolution

This project fundamentally shaped my approach to educational technology:

  1. Accessibility first - Education should not depend on expensive hardware
  2. Collaboration over isolation - Learning is inherently social
  3. Experience over memorization - Interactive understanding beats passive consumption
  4. Open over proprietary - Educational tools should empower, not restrict

Technical Achievements

Performance Optimizations

Frame Rate: Stable 60fps on mid-range hardware
Memory Usage: <500MB for complete VR experience
Cross-browser: Compatible with Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge

Accessibility Features

  • Keyboard navigation for all interactive elements
